About this prototype

Data: simulated ArmBar operating data, Jan 2025 – Jun 2026, 5 branches. Sources: Gym membership system, Bar point-of-sale, Finance expense sheet. Pipeline: generate_raw.py → prepare.py (cleaning, integration, star schema) → this dashboard. Revenue is recorded on a cash basis (membership fees count in the month paid).
