About ArmBar

Two businesses.
One roof.

ArmBar is headquartered in Roxas City, Capiz, with branches across Panay Island, Cebu and Manila. Every branch runs both a gym and a bar.

That means twice the data and twice the decisions. This project brings it all into one place, so management compares branches and business units with the same numbers.

ArmBar Gym

Memberships, personal training, group classes and day passes.

ArmBar Bar

Food, drinks and social space, right next to the gym floor.
